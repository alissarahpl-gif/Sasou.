About Me

I began with drawing, but over time I shifted my focus to painting, today I think of myself as a painter who experiments with many different types of mediums like pencil, pen, oil paint, acrylic, cardboard, and clay. Art has been a part of my life; I have always wanted to create in some way. Being creative is fun and a way that I can express myself as well as communicate with others; you can have a whole conversation with someone with just one painting, and I think that is amazing. I also really enjoy doing video art with some of my art pieces. In a lot of my work, I use mixed media. I do this because I like working with both paint and graphite and seeing how they look together. If I were to describe my art, I would say that it is a representation of what is going on in my mind. I use a lot of color, and I love bringing my own reference; something I put on canvas is very random, but I think that is what makes my work interesting.
